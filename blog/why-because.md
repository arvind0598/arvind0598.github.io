Because is a way of publishing: short ideas, each one answering a question that another idea raises. A blog can be more than a sequence of finished articles. It can also show how the ideas behind an investigation relate to each other, so that a complicated subject can be developed a piece at a time and a reader can see how the author reached their conclusions.

Because is not an argument against articles. It is an alternative way to develop, connect and publish the thinking that articles come from. This post explains why it exists, using the format it describes.
