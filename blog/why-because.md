Because is a new way of publishing. It involves multiple short "ideas", each one answering a question that another idea raises. This shows how the ideas behind an investigation relate to each other, so that a complicated subject can be developed a piece at a time and a reader can see how the author reached their conclusion.

Because is not an argument _against_ articles. It is an alternative way to develop and publish the thinking that articles come from. This post explains why it exists, using the format that it describes.
